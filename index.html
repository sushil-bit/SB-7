This is a fan-made guide, not an official Marvel publication. Confirmed release information is prioritized, while future connections are labelled as recommendations rather than facts. Marvel currently lists Avengers: Doomsday for December 18, 2026 and Avengers: Secret Wars for December 17, 2027.
